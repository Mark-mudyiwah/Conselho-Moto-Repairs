Why Regular Servicing Matters

Don't Wait Until Something Goes Wrong

Regular maintenance gives you an opportunity to identify worn or damaged components before they develop into larger problems.

For delivery riders, a reliable motorcycle is especially important because your bike is working for many hours and kilometres every day.

  • Helps maintain reliable performance
  • Helps identify worn components
  • Helps maintain engine performance
  • Can help identify problems early
  • Keeps your bike ready for daily work
Know The Signs

Your Bike May Be Due For A Service

Poor Performance

The motorcycle feels slower, less responsive or different from normal.

Unusual Noises

New or unusual engine sounds may indicate that the motorcycle needs inspection.

Starting Problems

Difficulty starting the bike can be a sign that certain components need attention.

Service Interval

If your bike has reached its recommended service interval, it may be time for maintenance.
