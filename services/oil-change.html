Engine Care

Give Your Engine Fresh Protection

Engine oil plays an important role in keeping your motorcycle's moving components lubricated and protected from excessive friction and heat.

Over time, oil becomes contaminated and loses some of its ability to protect the engine. A clogged or dirty oil filter can also reduce the effectiveness of the lubrication system.

Our oil and filter change service removes the old oil, replaces the filter where required, and refills your motorcycle with suitable fresh oil.

Why It Matters

Don't Ignore Your Engine Oil

Your motorcycle engine contains many moving parts that operate at high speeds and temperatures. Proper lubrication helps reduce friction and protects these components.

Regular oil changes are especially important for delivery bikes and motorcycles that spend many hours on the road.

  • Helps reduce engine friction
  • Helps keep engine components lubricated
  • Helps remove contaminants
  • Supports consistent engine performance
  • Helps maintain long-term engine health
Know The Signs

Your Bike May Be Due For An Oil Change

Engine Running Rough

Your engine may feel less smooth or responsive than usual.

Excessive Heat

An engine that is running hotter than usual may require inspection.

Oil Looks Dirty

Dark or contaminated oil can indicate that the oil is due for replacement.

Service Interval

If your motorcycle has reached its recommended service interval, it's time for a check-up.
