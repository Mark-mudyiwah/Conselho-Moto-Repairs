General Motorcycle Repairs

Something Not Running Right?

Not every motorcycle problem fits into a specific service. Sometimes your bike simply doesn't sound, feel or perform the way it should.

Our general repair service is designed for those problems. We inspect the motorcycle, identify the likely cause and recommend the appropriate repair.

From unusual noises and oil leaks to starting problems, damaged components and mechanical faults, we'll help determine what your motorcycle needs.

General Repairs

Problems We Can Help With

Our general repair service covers a wide range of mechanical and electrical problems that may affect your motorcycle.

Fault Finding

We investigate unusual symptoms, noises, performance problems and other faults to identify where the problem may be coming from.

Mechanical Repairs

Worn, damaged or loose mechanical components can be inspected and repaired or replaced where appropriate.

Electrical Repairs

We can investigate basic electrical faults involving wiring, switches, lights, starting and charging systems.

Oil & Fluid Leaks

Leaks are inspected to help identify their source and determine which seals, hoses, gaskets or components may require attention.

Overheating Problems

Cooling-related problems can be investigated when a motorcycle runs hotter than normal or shows signs of overheating.

Component Replacement

Worn or damaged components can be replaced where suitable parts are available and the repair is approved.

Before The Repair

Diagnose The Problem Before Replacing Parts

A motorcycle can show similar symptoms for completely different reasons. A starting problem, for example, could be related to the battery, starter system, fuel delivery, ignition or another component.

That's why we inspect the motorcycle and investigate the symptoms before recommending unnecessary parts or repairs.

  • Listen to the customer's symptoms
  • Visual inspection
  • Mechanical checks
  • Basic electrical checks
  • Identify the likely cause
  • Recommend the appropriate repair
Common Repair Requests

Is Your Bike Doing Something Strange?

If your motorcycle has developed a problem that you cannot identify, these are some of the common symptoms we can help investigate.

Strange Noises

Knocking, rattling, grinding, clicking or other unusual sounds that were not previously present.

Starting Problems

The motorcycle struggles to start, takes multiple attempts or suddenly refuses to start.

Fluid Leaks

Oil, coolant or other fluids appearing underneath or around the motorcycle.

Poor Performance

Loss of power, hesitation, rough running or a motorcycle that no longer performs as it normally should.

Electrical Faults

Problems with lights, indicators, switches, starting, charging or other electrical components.

Warning Signs

New smells, overheating, vibrations, warning lights or other changes that may indicate a developing problem.

Motorcycle Inspection

We Check The Important Areas

Depending on the symptoms, we focus our inspection on the systems most likely to be responsible for the problem.

Engine

Investigation of unusual engine noises, poor performance, starting problems, leaks and other engine-related symptoms.

Electrical System

Basic checks of the battery, wiring, switches, lights, charging and starting circuits.

Drivetrain

Inspection of chains, sprockets and related drivetrain components when unusual movement or power-transfer problems are reported.

Cooling System

Cooling components can be inspected when the motorcycle is overheating or running at abnormal temperatures.

Cables & Controls

Throttle, clutch, brake cables and other controls can be checked for damage, stiffness or incorrect operation.

General Components

Loose, worn or damaged components are inspected when they may be contributing to the reported problem.

For Working Motorcycles

Keep Your Delivery Bike Working

Delivery motorcycles spend long hours on the road and are exposed to constant starting, stopping, traffic, potholes and heavy daily use.

Small problems can quickly become expensive downtime if they are ignored. General repairs help address problems before they become bigger failures.

  • Identify problems early
  • Reduce unnecessary downtime
  • Keep important components working properly
  • Improve reliability for daily riding
  • Address unusual symptoms before they worsen
  • Get professional repair recommendations
