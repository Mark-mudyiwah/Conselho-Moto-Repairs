Why It Matters

A Healthy Suspension Improves Control

Motorcycle suspension helps keep the tyres in contact with the road while absorbing bumps and changes in the road surface.

Worn or damaged suspension components can affect braking, cornering, stability and rider comfort, especially on motorcycles that are used every day.

  • Better motorcycle stability
  • Improved handling and cornering
  • More controlled braking
  • Better rider comfort
  • Improved wheel stability
  • Early detection of worn components
Know The Signs

Your Suspension or Wheels May Need Attention

If your motorcycle has started handling differently or making unusual noises, these may be signs that the suspension or wheels need to be inspected.

Excessive Bouncing

The motorcycle continues bouncing after going over bumps instead of settling normally.

Fork Oil Leaks

Oil around the front fork tubes can indicate a damaged seal or another suspension problem.

Wheel Play

Noticeable movement in the wheel can indicate worn bearings or loose wheel components.

Unusual Noises

Knocking, grinding or clicking sounds may indicate worn suspension, bearings or other wheel components.

Poor Handling

The motorcycle feels unstable, wanders or does not feel as planted as it normally does.

Damaged Rim

A bent, cracked or damaged rim can affect wheel rotation, handling and overall riding safety.

Common Problems

Problems We Can Help Diagnose

Suspension and wheel problems can develop gradually, especially on motorcycles used every day for commuting or delivery work.

  • Worn wheel bearings
  • Leaking fork seals
  • Worn or damaged shocks
  • Bent or damaged rims
  • Loose wheel components
  • Suspension knocking or unusual noises
