Why Proper Installation Matters

Secure Your Delivery Setup

Delivery boxes and carriers are exposed to vibration, bumps and movement every time you ride. A poorly mounted setup can become loose and affect the stability of the motorcycle.

Proper positioning and secure mounting help reduce unnecessary movement and give you greater confidence while carrying deliveries throughout the day.

  • Secure carrier mounting
  • Stable delivery box installation
  • Correct positioning and alignment
  • Reduced movement and vibration
  • Suitable setup for daily deliveries
Installation Warning Signs

Is Your Delivery Setup Secure Enough?

These are some common signs that your carrier or delivery box may need inspection or adjustment.

Box Is Moving

Excessive movement or shaking while riding may indicate loose mounting hardware or an unstable installation.

Loose Bolts

Loose or missing bolts can reduce the strength and stability of the carrier and delivery box.

Uneven Position

A carrier or box that sits incorrectly may need repositioning and adjustment before continued use.

Damaged Mounts

Cracked brackets, damaged mounting points or worn hardware should be inspected before the setup is used again.
